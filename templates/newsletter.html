Three reader tips worth stealing

1. One basket by the door. Keys, mail, and chargers land in one place instead of five.
2. Label the shelf, not the box. Labels where you look, not where you store, get used longer.
3. A five minute reset beats a Saturday clean. Small daily resets keep small spaces calm.
